The problem

The number is one line.
The phone makes it twenty steps.

Checking is a chore

Unlock, open the app, wait for the feed, find your market, read the number. Thirty seconds of friction for something that takes one second to understand.

So people guess instead

Most checks never happen. Decisions get made on yesterday's price, a headline, or a feeling — while the market moved hours ago.

Acting is even slower

By the time you have opened a position screen, the moment you wanted to act on has usually passed. The glance and the action live in different places.

The solution

Read it on the glasses.
Act on it from the glasses.

1

Glance

Your watched markets and the day's biggest movers, one line each: the price, the move, the question. Live from Polymarket, refreshed every 30 seconds.

2

Tap

One tap opens a market. One more places a paper bet at the live price, sets an alert, or adds it to your watchlist. Double-tap goes back — or steps the bet size up.

3

Get told

Set a line — say, Yes at 65%. When the market crosses it while the app is open, the alert takes over the display. And the watcher that ships with the app can send the same crossing to your Telegram when the app is closed — see the panel under the simulator.

Voice works the same way: say next, watch this, bet ten on yes, positions, back. In this browser demo the mic button uses your browser's speech recognition where it offers one; the same commands sit under the display as chips, so every command can be tried either way.

Telegram alerts — for when the app is closed

An alert set here only fires while this app is open — the glasses show nothing while another app is in front. The watcher that ships with this project (the watcher/ folder in the repo) closes that gap: it checks the same price lines on a timer and sends the crossing to your Telegram, app open or not.

When live odds load, the Telegram command for a market appears here.

The build is covered by 38 automated tests: the core maths and screen logic, pinned to a recorded response from the live Polymarket API, plus the Telegram watcher and the board maths, tested with fake state so no test touches the live network. If a fetch fails, the display keeps the last good numbers and says so — it never invents a price.

PolyGlance — built for Heads Up: Even Realities (DoraHacks), Track 02 · Crypto & DeFi. Data: Polymarket Gamma API, public and live. Odds are market prices, not advice; nothing here is financial advice.

Paper trading only: no wallet, no real orders, no user funds — as the event rules require. Alerts in the app work while it is open; the G2 cannot show anything while another app is in front, and PolyGlance does not pretend otherwise. The optional Telegram watcher covers the closed-app case: it checks the same lines and messages you, and it is read-only like the app.
