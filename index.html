The problem

The number is one line.
The phone makes it twenty steps.

Checking is a chore

Unlock, open the app, wait for the feed, find your market, read the number. Thirty seconds of friction for something that takes one second to understand.

So people guess instead

Most checks never happen. Decisions get made on yesterday's price, a headline, or a feeling — while the market moved hours ago.

Acting is even slower

By the time you have opened a position screen, the moment you wanted to act on has usually passed. The glance and the action live in different places.

The solution

Read it on the glasses.
Act on it from the glasses.

1

Glance

Your watched markets and the day's biggest movers, one line each: the price, the move, the question. Live from Polymarket, refreshed every 30 seconds.

2

Tap

One tap opens a market. One more places a paper bet at the live price, sets an alert, or adds it to your watchlist. Double-tap goes back — or steps the bet size up.

3

Get told

Set a line — say, Yes at 65%. When the market crosses it while the app is open, the alert takes over the display. No phone buzz, no missed move.

Voice works the same way: say next, watch this, bet ten on yes, positions, back. In this browser demo the mic button uses your browser's speech recognition where it offers one; the same commands sit under the display as chips, so every command can be tried either way.

real money at risk — every bet in PolyGlance is paper

The build is covered by 16 automated tests on the core maths and screen logic, run against a recorded response from the live Polymarket API. If a fetch fails, the display keeps the last good numbers and says so — it never invents a price.

PolyGlance — built for Heads Up: Even Realities (DoraHacks), Track 02 · Crypto & DeFi. Data: Polymarket Gamma API, public and live. Odds are market prices, not advice; nothing here is financial advice.

Paper trading only: no wallet, no real orders, no user funds — as the event rules require. Alerts work while the app is open; the G2 cannot show anything while another app is in front, and PolyGlance does not pretend otherwise.
